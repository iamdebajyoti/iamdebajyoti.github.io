### User Management
> User IDs on AS/400 (IBM i) systems are stored within the OS/400 user profile objects, which can be queried using commands like DSPUSRPRF or WRKUSRPRF.



To scroll through screens or subfiles wider than your display—such as viewing columns shifted off the edge of the screen—use the system function keys F19 (Scroll Left) and F20 (Scroll Right).

> Because AS/400 terminals emulate a classic 132-column or 80-column green screen, you cannot "smooth scroll" with a mouse wheel like you do in Windows. You have to jump by pages or increments:F19 (or Shift + F7): Scroll LeftF20 (or Shift + F8): Scroll RightFor navigation inside input-capable fields or prompts:Tab: Jump forward from left to right (field to field).Shift + Tab: Jump backward from right to left.Field Exit: Clears all data to the right of your cursor.
